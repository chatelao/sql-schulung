# 200-Lesson Pacing & Delivery Schedules

Each lesson is **45 minutes long**, totaling **150 clock hours** across **10 Phases** and **6 Identical Checkpoints (`CP-0`–`CP-5`)**.

---

## Choose Your Pacing Track

### Track 1: Intensive Full-Time Bootcamp (5 Weeks)
- **Cadence**: 8 lessons/day (6 clock hours/day), 5 days/week = **40 lessons/week**.
- **Week 1 (`L001–L040`)**: Phase 1 + Phase 2 (up to `DEFINE`/`VERIFY`)
- **Week 2 (`L041–L080`)**: `CP-1`, Phase 3 (Functions), Phase 4 (Grouping)
- **Week 3 (`L081–L120`)**: `CP-2`, Phase 5 (Joins), Phase 6 (Subqueries & `SET` Operators)
- **Week 4 (`L121–L160`)**: `CP-3`, Phase 7 (DML/TCL), Phase 8 (DDL/Constraints/Views/Indexes)
- **Week 5 (`L161–L200`)**: `CP-4`, Phase 9 (Data Dictionary/Time Zones/DCL), Phase 10 (Mocks & `CP-5`)

### Track 2: Part-Time Evening / Vocational Course (20 Weeks)
- **Cadence**: 10 lessons/week (e.g., Tue & Thu evenings × 3 lessons + Saturday × 4 lessons).
- Ideal for working professionals transitioning into data, QA, or Oracle development roles.

### Track 3: Academic / Self-Paced School Year (34 Weeks)
- **Cadence**: 6 lessons/week (4.5 hours/week).
- Leaves natural breathing room for weekly spaced-repetition flashcards on GitHub Pages.

## Checkpoint Schedule Across the 200 Lessons

| Checkpoint | Lessons | Timing | What Happens |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **CP-0** | `L001–L002` | Start of Phase 1 | Honest zero-knowledge baseline (no studying beforehand). |
| **CP-1** | `L041–L042` | End of Phase 2 | Measures Domains 1–2 gains + confidence calibration. |
| **CP-2** | `L081–L082` | End of Phase 4 | Measures huge jump from Domain 3 (25% exam weight). |
| **CP-3** | `L121–L122` | End of Phase 6 | Target milestone: crossing the **65%** exam threshold. |
| **CP-4** | `L161–L162` | End of Phase 8 | Full SQL query + DDL/DML fluency check (**75%+** target). |
| **CP-5** | `L199–L200` | End of Phase 10 | Final benchmark (**≥ 85%**) + full answer key unlocked! |
